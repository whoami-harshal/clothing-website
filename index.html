Fabric

The weight of a good weave

Hold a length of khadi to the light and you'll see it — the slubs, the small unevenness of thread spun by hand. That is the cloth telling you it is real.

Heritage & philosophy

Four decades of quiet devotion

In 1982, Swadeshi Khadi Bhandar opened its doors in Kandivali West. Swadeshi — of our own country. The name was a promise, and the shelves have kept it.
