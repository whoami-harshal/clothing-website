Our story

Four decades of pure handloom

In 1982, Swadeshi Khadi Bhandar opened on S.V. Road in Kandivali West. Swadeshi means "of our own country" — and the shop has kept that promise ever since, with cloth spun and woven by hand.

Today the shelves still hold khadi by the metre in every shade, ready-made kurtas and shirts, sarees, herbal soaps and pure honey — and the same welcome at the counter.

Why khadi

Cloth with a conscience

Handwoven

Spun and woven by hand, so every metre carries small, honest irregularities that machine cloth can't copy.

Sustainable

Hand-spinning and hand-weaving use far less power than mill production, and keep village weavers at work.

Pure & Natural

Breathable cotton that stays cool through a Mumbai summer and softens with every wash.
